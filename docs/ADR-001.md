# ADR-001 — The LegalByte domain is centered on Requests

## Context

The domain was initially modeled around Executions. After analyzing the
business workflow, we realized that users do not think in terms of
"executions." They submit requests for the platform to solve a problem.

## Decision

The central domain entity will be `Request`.

A Request may generate one or more Tasks, which may use one or more
Automations for processing.

## Consequences

- The domain is aligned with the language of the business.
- Automation implementation remains an internal detail.
- A Request may be fulfilled by one or more Tasks.
- New processing mechanisms may be added without changing the central domain
  concept.
